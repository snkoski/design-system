# Voice and copy

- Write in first person ("I build…") on personal sites. Address the reader as "you."
- Sentence case everywhere: headings, buttons, and labels. Uppercase only in `label` eyebrows.
- Always use the Oxford comma: "tangerine, peach, and mint."
- Short, plain sentences. Humor goes in headlines, empty states, and asides, never in error messages, legal text, or anything a stressed person reads.
- Buttons say exactly what happens: "Send message", then a toast that says "Message sent."
- No emoji as decoration or section markers.
- Example headline: "Strange ideas, carefully made." Example empty state: "Nothing here yet. The hot dogs are still grilling."
